We added TOTP autofill, support for PIN resources, and a new expert setting to reduce re-authentication prompts. Orientation lock has been relaxed, so the app now adapts to screen orientation on large screens with improved split-screen behaviour. On the security side, the pwned password check is back, and we've introduced Gradle lock files to harden the build. We also fixed a foreground service crash and an overlapping MFA keyboard, and improved UX on the resource list preventing list jumps.
